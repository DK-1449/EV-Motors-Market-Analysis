# 🚗 EV Motors Market Analysis - Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an in-depth **Electric Vehicle (EV) Sales Analysis** across Indian states and manufacturers, using Power BI. It includes interactive dashboards for visualizing trends, top performers, and EV penetration metrics.

## Problem Statement
AtliQ Motors, a leading automotive company from the USA specializing in electric vehicles (EVs), has achieved a 25% market share in the electric and hybrid vehicle segment in North America over the last 5 years. As they plan to expand their presence in India, where their market share is currently less than 2%, they needed a comprehensive analysis of the Indian EV/Hybrid market to strategize their launch effectively.

## Data Overview
- Dataset 1: dim_date (Date, Fiscal_Year, Year, Month, Quarter)
- Dataset 2: EV Sales by Makers (Date,EVs sold, Maker, Vehicle_Category)
- Dataset 3: EV Sales by State (Date,EVs sold,State,Total_Vehicle_Sold, Vehicle_Category)

## 💡 Insights
## State-Wise EV Sales Trends:
- Total vehicle sales across all states: 57M
- Total EV sales: 2M, with EV penetration rate at 3.61%, indicating slow adoption.
- Top 5 states with the highest EV sales:
- Maharashtra (396K), Karnataka (313K), Tamil Nadu (200K), Gujarat (181K), Uttar Pradesh (95K).
- EV 2-Wheeler sales dominate over 4-Wheelers (2M vs. 153K).
- Market Trends Over Time:
- Overall vehicle sales saw fluctuations but peaked at 2.6M in 2023, declining slightly in 2024.
- EV sales increased steadily, peaking at 133K in 2024, indicating growing adoption
  
---

## Makers’ Market Share Analysis:
- Total EV sales by makers: 140M, with 130M in 2-Wheelers and 10M in 4-Wheelers.
- Top 5 EV 2-Wheeler makers:
- Ola Electric (33M) leads the market, followed by TVS (18M), Ather (14M), Hero Electric (12M), and Ampere (11M).
- Top EV 4-Wheeler makers:
- Mahindra & Mahindra (2.8M, 27.8%), Tata Motors (6M, 59.91%), and MG Motor (933K, 9.27%).
- EV market penetration is 245.04, indicating a higher concentration of sales among a few key players.
  
---

## Recommendations for AtliQ Motors' Entry Strategy in India
- ✅ Target the top-performing states: Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu have high EV adoption rates. A focused marketing and dealership expansion in these regions can 
- help gain traction.
- ✅ Prioritize 2-Wheelers before 4-Wheelers: With 2M EV 2-wheeler sales vs. 153K EV 4-wheeler sales, AtliQ should initially launch electric scooters and bikes before - - --- expanding into cars.
- ✅ Competitive Positioning:
- Ola Electric and TVS dominate the 2-Wheeler segment. AtliQ should differentiate through better battery technology, pricing, and after-sales service.
- Tata Motors and Mahindra control the 4-Wheeler space. AtliQ should focus on SUVs or affordable electric cars to capture market share.

## 📌 Conclusion
- India’s EV market is growing but still at an early stage, with a penetration rate of just 3.61%.
- The 2-Wheeler segment dominates, making it a strategic entry point for AtliQ Motors.
- Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu are key markets with high EV adoption rates.
- Strong competition exists from Ola, TVS, Tata, and Mahindra, so AtliQ needs to focus on differentiation, affordability, and partnerships.
- Investing in charging infrastructure, leveraging government incentives, and aggressive marketing will help AtliQ Motors establish a strong foothold in India![image]
